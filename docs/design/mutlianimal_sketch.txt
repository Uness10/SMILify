- Data representation change:
    sample= V images,
             animal_i: V*55 2D keypoints, params

- #queries change : N queries :  query i -> animal i
    qi attends to the image and extract info belonging to 1 animal (e.g DETR-style object queries)
- Parameter(pose,shape ..) Head becomes per-animal 
    -> Camera params are diff! animals are in the same camera (Scene level params Vs Animal level params)

- Multi view becomes more complicated: Try single view first

- Animal matching problem: GT= (anml1,anml2,anml3) Vs predicted=(anml3,anml1,anml2)
    A naive loss would do GT[i] <-> predicted[i]
    -> establish a fixed identity/ordering beforehand.
-> Enforce a strict matching of one head associated with one specimen in the footage, so we don't deal with identity ambiguity

- Occlusion Problem: visible animal, partially visible animal,heavily occluded animal
